About the hall

Part of Our Lady of the Assumption Church.

The hall sits alongside Our Lady of the Assumption Church on Bath Road, and has been a fixture of parish and community life in Thatcham for years — from Sunday coffee after Mass to children's parties, craft fairs and committee meetings.

It's a genuinely big space: a stage, twenty-five tables, seating for around 150, and a kitchen kitted out for serving a crowd — oven, fridge, freezer, urn, and enough cups and saucers that nobody's washing up mid-event.
